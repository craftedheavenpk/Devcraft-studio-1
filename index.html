FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a website take?

The proposal estimates around 9–10 working days after the advance and all required content are received. Daraz setup is estimated at 10–15 days. Complex projects may take longer.

What are your payment terms?

The attached agreement proposes 50% advance before work begins and the remaining 50% after demo review, before final domain mapping or handover.

Do you provide a domain and hosting?

Free hosting/subdomain options may be available with limitations. A custom domain, renewals, paid hosting and third-party services are additional unless included in writing.

How many revisions are included?

Your agreement includes up to 4 reasonable design/content feedback rounds during development unless your selected package or written brief states otherwise.

Can I request edits after launch?

15 days of post-launch technical support for bugs and minor tweaks within the delivered scope are included. New features and major changes may cost extra.

How do I place an order?

Choose a plan, fill in the project brief and press “Send order on WhatsApp”. We will review the details and confirm scope, payment and schedule before work begins.
